Privacy policy

Jainism F.A.Q.s · abhijithda.github.io/jainism · Last updated 24 November 2026

In short

  • This is a free, non-commercial reading site. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.
  • Your reading progress, study answers and display settings live in your own browser.
  • If you choose Sign in with Google, that same information is also saved only in the hidden app-data folder (appDataFolder) of your own Google Drive, so it follows you to your other devices.
  • The site's owner never receives, collects, reads or stores that information. It goes between your browser and your Google Drive, and nowhere else.

Without signing in

Everything you do in the book (the pages you mark as read, the study questions you answer, your language, font size, paper size and other display choices) is stored in your browser's local storage on your device. It is not sent to the site. Clearing your browser data for this site removes it.

If you sign in with Google (optional)

What the site asks Google for. One permission only: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata. It lets this site read and write a single hidden folder in your Google Drive that is reserved for the site. It does not let the site see, list, open or change any of your other Drive files, and the hidden folder does not appear in your normal Drive.

What is saved there. One small file, jainism-progress.json, containing:

  • which parts of the book you have marked as read (their internal ids) and when you changed each one;
  • which study questions you have answered and whether you marked them “got it” or “review later”, as short descriptors (not the book's text);
  • a few display choices: languages, videos on or off, QR codes on or off, read tracking, study companion, paper size and font size.

Settings that belong to a device (the offline copy switch and everything about the AI assistant, including any addresses or keys you enter) are never saved to Drive.

Your name and email. After you sign in, the site asks Google who you are, only to show “Synced as you@example.com” in Settings. That label is kept in your browser's local storage on your device and is not sent anywhere else.

Where it goes. Directly from your browser to Google Drive using Google's sign-in. There is no server run by this site in between. The site's owner cannot see your Google account, your Drive, or what is in the file.

Sharing and use. The information is used only to show the same reading progress and settings on your devices. It is not sold, shared, rented or transferred to anyone, not used for advertising, and not read by people.

Google API Services User Data Policy. This site's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Stopping and deleting

  • Sign out in Settings (or on the Home page). Your reading history stays on this device, and the site's access is revoked with Google.
  • Delete cloud copy in the same place signs you out and also deletes jainism-progress.json from your Drive.
  • You can also remove the site's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete its hidden data in Google Drive under Settings → Manage apps.

Other parts of the site

  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may keep standard server logs under GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Videos. Reference videos play on YouTube when you press play, under YouTube's own terms and privacy policy.
  • AI assistant (optional). If you use it, the question you type is sent to produce the answer: with the Cloud engine, through a Cloudflare Worker to an AI model; with the Local or Custom engine, to the address you set. Signing in with Google does not involve the assistant, and nothing in your Drive file is sent to it.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version and its date will appear on this page. Questions or requests: please open an issue on the project's GitHub page.
